# 3-Wheeler EV Adoption Analysis — India

Strategic market research and feasibility assessment of **electric 3-wheelers (E3Ws)** as a mainstream mobility solution for India: market sizing, regulatory landscape, MECE market structure, driver-level barriers and business models for faster adoption.

The quantitative analysis is built on **public Kaggle datasets** and is fully reproducible from a single notebook. The primary-research layer (interviews with 3-wheeler drivers) is supported by a coding template and analysis code; raw interview data is **not** published in this repository.

## Business questions

1. How large is the E3W market in India and how fast is it growing?
2. Where is adoption concentrated (states), and how concentrated is it?
3. Does an EV pay off for a driver compared with a CNG/ICE 3-wheeler?
4. What barriers do drivers report, and which business models address them?

## Approach

| Step | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pull state × month × vehicle-class EV sales and charging-station data from Kaggle | `notebooks/01_ev_3w_adoption_analysis.ipynb` §2 |
| 2 | Clean and standardise; flag partial years so growth metrics are not distorted | §3 |
| 3 | **MECE** segmentation of the electric 3W market (passenger / cargo, state, time) with a built-in exhaustiveness check | §4 |
| 4 | Trend, YoY growth, CAGR, share of total EV sales, seasonality | §5 |
| 5 | State-level volume, concentration (top-5 share, HHI), momentum heatmap | §6 |
| 6 | Charging infrastructure vs. adoption (Spearman rank correlation) | §7 |
| 7 | Unit economics: running cost and payback vs. CNG, with sensitivity analysis | §8 |
| 8 | Regulatory landscape (central + state instruments) | §9 |
| 9 | Driver-interview coding → ranked barriers | §10 |
| 10 | Business models mapped to barriers and evidence | §11 |

## Data sources

| Dataset | Used for | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Electric Vehicle Sales by State in India | State/month/class EV sales | https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mafzal19/electric-vehicle-sales-by-state-in-india |
| Electric Vehicle Charging Stations in India | Charging infrastructure by state | https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/saketpradhan/electric-vehicle-charging-stations-in-india |
| Primary research | Interviews with 3-wheeler drivers | Real data not included (privacy). `interviews_synthetic_demo.csv` is randomly generated demo data only — see `data/interviews/README.md` |

Datasets are downloaded at run time and are **not** committed (`data/raw/` is git-ignored). Please check each dataset's licence on its Kaggle page before reusing it.

Possible extension for a true penetration metric (EV share of *all* 3W sales): join fuel-wise registration data such as *Veh Registrations in India from 2020 to 2024* (`npsingh90/veh-registrations-in-india-from-2020-to-2024`) or *Indian Vehicle Registration Data (2020–25)* (`aatifahmad123/indian-vehicle-registration-data-202025`).

## Limitations

- Sales data are registration-based, so informal / unregistered e-rickshaws are likely under-counted.
- Unit-economics inputs in section 8 are assumptions; replace them with sourced values and read the sensitivity table rather than a single base case.
- Interview findings are qualitative and directional, not statistically representative. The bundled interview file is synthetic demo data, not research evidence.
- Policy details (subsidy rates, caps, end dates) change frequently; verify against official notifications.
